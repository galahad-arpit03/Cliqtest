Chapter 01

The "Dark Matter" Interface Theory

Enterprise software historically relies on glaring white backgrounds. For cliQTest, this paradigm is inverted. Our users—Automation Engineers and DevOps professionals—analyze dense telemetry for hours. White backgrounds induce cognitive fatigue.

The Abyss & The Surface

Our architecture begins at absolute zero: #000000. Nothing lives here except space. Interactive elements live on the "Surface" (#0A0A0A). Because drop-shadows are invisible in the dark, elevation is achieved entirely through Chromatic Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
